The inherited MIT license applies to project software/documentation. It does not override third-party rights in NHIS, KOSIS, resident-population, survey or other source statistics, nor license omitted geometry. No new CC0/CC-BY grant is asserted for the aggregate panel or derived result tables.

The supplied panel is analysis-ready district-year aggregate data; source definitions, coverage, transformations and exclusions are in `data/info.json`, `data/meta.csv`, configuration and the archived code. Users should consult each original provider's applicable terms before redistribution or other reuse. This package does not assert that all raw source redistribution rights were independently verified. Raw licensed shapefiles and unrelated multi-disease MASTER data are excluded.
